## Draft (EN)

> I spent an afternoon vibe coding a little Android app called **BrainWatch** 🧠 — a
> dopamine-fatigue tracker for Instagram. No AndroidX, no libraries, no backend.
> Just the raw Android SDK, an accessibility service, a Canvas-drawn brain and a
> lot of "one more feature" energy.
>
> It watches *how my thumb scrolls* and turns it into something useful:
> • Live scroll / reel / tap counting (accessibility events, debounced & impulse-grouped)
> • Animated brain that sags, droops and dozes off as my reward system drains
> • A floating chip over Instagram with a green→red "heat" dot per scroll
> • A break reminder I can set to ANY time (hours : minutes : seconds) that pops a
>   check-in card — then gets me out of the app when I say so
> • Home-screen widget + full session history. 100% on-device, nothing leaves the phone.
>
> The best part about vibe coding? You ship. It won't be perfect — but it works and
> it's mine. Fixed real bugs along the way (parameter-order bugs are a vibe-coder's
> rite of passage 😅).
>
> → Repo/APK: [link]
> → Built with raw Java + Gradle, minSdk 24, zero dependencies.
